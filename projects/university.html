Media Design School · 2017 – 2019

University projects

Team projects from my Bachelor of Software Engineering at Media Design School, mostly as the primary programmer. Two grew into bigger things: Mythos Party and UnderForge.

  • HL

    Hardlight

    A first-person shooter: defend a hacking station from waves of AI with a rifle, a pistol, a grenade, a shield and a propulsion device, while parkouring, wall-running and sliding. Shoot off robot limbs and absorb their energy for your weapons.

    Primary programmer: the player and movement, weapons, the game loop and rounds, and the UI.

    itch.io ↗Unreal Engine 4
  • ST

    Stolen Time

    A 2D platformer built in 3D: wall jumps and a dash, plus time powers that freeze or reverse moving platforms, as a projectile or an area effect.

    Primary programmer: the player, wall jumping, the boost, the time abilities, platforming level-design tools, the camera and rooms.

    itch.io ↗Unreal Engine 4
  • TP

    TossPots

    A co-op platformer where one player is Toss and one is Pot: Toss can pick up and throw Pot, but Pot is smaller and can jump. A hub level picks the level when both players walk in.

    Primary programmer: the hub and level select, UI, both players and their interactions, and puzzle tools for the level designer.

    Unreal Engine 4
  • PW

    Painted Wings

    Made for a brief on a watercolour art style: jump, glide and dash through two levels, collecting blueberries and dragonflies.

    Primary programmer: the player, platforming systems, game loops and collectables.

    itch.io ↗Unreal Engine 4
  • PO

    Portal

    A portal system inspired by Valve's Portal, built to learn camera and portal effects: shoot portals, see the right views through them, walk through, and use cubes to finish levels.

    Everything, including modelling the cube, the cube dropper and the two buttons.

    Unreal Engine 4
