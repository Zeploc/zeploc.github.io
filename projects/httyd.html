Project · Personal project · fan project

HTTYD

A dragon flight game in Unreal Engine 5, inspired by How to Train Your Dragon: you fly Toothless. I took it from placeholder shapes in a desert to an animated dragon soaring through a mountain range.

Type
Personal fan project
Engine
Unreal Engine 5
Focus
Flight feel and animation
Tools
Unreal, Blender

What I did

  • Flight model, tuned with placeholder shapes before the dragon existed
  • Animating Toothless in Blender
  • Animation Blueprint and blend spaces in Unreal
  • A mountain range landscape to fly through
