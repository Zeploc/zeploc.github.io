Client · Buildmedia

Spark 5G Race Zone: Boat Builder

Build and customise a boat on a tablet, see how well it will perform, then watch it race on the big screen. The second experience for Spark and Emirates Team New Zealand at the 2020 America's Cup.

Client
Spark · Emirates Team New Zealand
Studio
Buildmedia
Event
America's Cup 2020
Engine
Unreal Engine 4

How it works

Streamed to tablets
Unreal streamed in real time to more than ten tablets in the space.
Performance from parts
Each configuration changes aspects of the boat's performance, and the visuals change with it through skeletal meshes and morph targets.
Styles
Pick a full style or style each part.
Results
A dynamic sequence shows how well the boat does.
RFID badges
Scanning a badge saves the boat to a server through a REST API.
The big screen
A projector PC cycles through configured boats racing, with styled, dynamic camera angles.
