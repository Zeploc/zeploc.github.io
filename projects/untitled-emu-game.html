Untitled Emu Game

A team game made over one weekend: play an emu and save the eggs from the poachers before they drive off with them.

Type
Team project
Made
24 – 26 January 2025
Engine
Unreal Engine 5
Plugin
Sky Ability System

About the project

Eggs parachute onto the map. Get close to one and an arrow points the way, but the poachers start hunting for it too. Carry eggs back to the collection site to score, and bump a poacher's car to take an egg back. Commuters and conservationists share the roads.

What I built

  • The egg-poaching game mode, with nests, drop-off zones and the saved eggs on show at each base
  • An egg holder component for picking up, carrying and stealing eggs
  • Car AI that follows the roads to its target; poachers only hunt once you find an egg, and speed up when they do
  • Hilux parts that break off when hit hard enough
  • A cinematic camera that zooms out as you speed up, plus a ragdoll and "play dead" for the emu
  • Eggs that parachute in, a turret, the menu and controller support
