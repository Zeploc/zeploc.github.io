Lifeline

A space survival game where your station is your lifeline. Keep the air pressurised, the power flowing and yourself fed and watered, then bolt on scanners and harvesters to push further out.

Type
Personal project
Genre
Space survival
Engine
Unreal Engine 5
Status
In development

About the project

Subnautica's explore-and-survive loop, except the thing keeping you alive is something you built, and every system on it can fail.

— The pitch

What it does

  • Build and extend your own station or ship (still deciding which)
  • Life-support systems that really run: air pressure, power, food and water
  • Add-ons to build and expand: scanning equipment, resource gathering and more
