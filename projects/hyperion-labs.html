Hyperion Labs

A VR game prototype set in a high-tech lab: design mechanical gadgets at a holographic table, then build them and put them to the test.

About the project

The idea is a lab like Tony Stark’s: an open, techy space full of holographic tables. You design electronic and mechanical tech at a holographic table, and the lab builds it for you to test.

You weld parts together and connect them with joints: ball joints, slide rails, hinges, springs, hydraulics and stabilisers. Each joint gets a range of motion, driven or free. Materials have their own properties, and a design fails its test if a constraint breaks. Parts you make can be saved as templates and reused.

What I built so far

  • The lab map, with a circular workstation in the centre
  • VR hands in their own Blueprint, split out from the player pawn
  • A grabbable component for objects you can pick up
  • Joining objects with physics constraints (in progress)

Next on the list

  • Grabbing, first with a simple attach, then perhaps with physics constraints
  • Changing a part’s base shape, resizing it and welding parts together
  • Ball joints with free rotation, slide rails and hinges
  • A holographic material, and music playing in the lab
