Spark 5G Race Zone: Simulator

For Spark's 5G experience with Emirates Team New Zealand at the 2020 America's Cup: sail ETNZ's racing yacht between the goals like the real race, with a second player on the foils and rudder.

Client
Spark · Emirates Team New Zealand
Studio
Buildmedia
Event

Engine

How it works

Three screens
A three-screen setup using nDisplay.
Live link to ETNZ's simulator
ETNZ's internal simulator talked to Unreal over UDP, for a live, visually immersive version of it.
Game systems
Our own game systems on top: goals to sail between, and a second player on the foils and rudder.
