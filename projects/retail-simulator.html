Retail Simulator

A chaotic co-op management game where players run a busy retail store: keep the shelves stocked, serve customers and catch shoplifters, with physics-based chaos and local or online multiplayer.

Type
Personal project
Genre
Co-op management
Engine

Players
Local and online co-op

What it does

  • Stock shelves with forklifts and by hand
  • Serve customers at the register and the cafe
  • Clean spills, water plants and catch shoplifters
  • Store popularity that sets how chaotic each five-minute day gets
