Project · Skyward Studios · my studio

Mythos Party

An online multiplayer arena brawler on Steam with a collection of mythologies, where you fight other players for the glory of your chosen god. It is a team project that started at university, which we have kept developing at Skyward Studios.

About the project

Hack, slash, punch and slap your opponents for the glory of the gods and be rewarded with their divine power!

— Steam store page

What I did

  • Core systems, abilities, menus and UI, networking and weapons
  • The round-based game loop, using level streaming to switch maps
  • A custom 3D widget system for the loadout screen, menu and lobby
  • Steamworks integration: invites, game status and DLC unlocks
  • A party and lobby system with synced loadouts, chat, ready-up, and map and team selection
  • Custom synced network systems, so every player sees the same thing without desync
  • The scoreboard, chat, tribute carousel and end-game screen
  • A base player that works with AI bots, and abilities, weapons and Paragon mode that swap in and out

The game

  • Weapons and shields

    Weapons and a shield make up the base combat.

  • Tribute carousels

    As the rounds go on, players collect tributes from the gods to use in the fight.

  • Abilities and Paragon mode

    Successful combat charges abilities. Eliminations earn Paragon charges, which unlock a full god form with new abilities for a short time.

  • Greek mythology pack

    Three maps set in Olympus: Undergrowth, Mountain Pass and Peak.

  • Game modes

    Free-for-all and team play, each as deathmatch or stock (limited lives).

  • Training mode

    Practise with every weapon, ability and Paragon.

  • Customisation

    Eyes, facial hair and accessories, hats, torso, waist, shoes and back, each with custom colours, plus weapon and shield skins. Locked skins unlock through Steam DLC.

  • Lobbies and chat

    Host and join lobbies, show off skins before the game and chat.
