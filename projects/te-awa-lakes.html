Client · Buildmedia

Te Awa Lakes

An interactive masterplan and a VR hot-air balloon for a development near Hamilton: explore the community and its features on screen, or see it first hand from a balloon.

Client
Te Awa Lakes
Studio
Buildmedia
VR headset
Vive Pro 2
Engine
Unreal Engine 4

The masterplan

  • Free camera

    Full camera movement around the development.

  • Points of interest

    Locations of interest and zoom levels.

  • Time of day

    Change the time of day.

  • Measure tool

    Measure distances across the site.

  • Staged views

    See each stage of the development.

The VR balloon

  • Guided

    A journey through the development past points of interest, on a fixed path.

  • Free-roam

    Control the height like a real hot-air balloon, by adding gas or venting.

  • Look to steer

    Look at a location of interest to fly towards it, and the balloon then circles it.
