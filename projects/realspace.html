Client · Buildmedia

Realspace

A real-time, interactive experience that showcases and sells unbuilt property, through a simple touch interface or full immersion in a VR headset. We built it as a product of reusable systems, used across dozens of client projects.

Product
Realspace
Studio
Buildmedia
Role
One of the primary programmers
Engine
Unreal Engine 4

What I did

  • Reusable systems used in dozens of client projects
  • A single working project that distributes them all, using a custom-built external packaging tool

Features

  • Teleport

    Move around the property.

  • Plan view

    See the layout from above.

  • Measure tool

    Measure any space.

  • Gallery

    Images, plans and movies.

  • Cinematic photo mode

    Take cinematic photos.

  • Level and material switchers

    Switch building levels and materials.

  • Exterior

    View the development at a high level, then filter down to an apartment to explore.

  • VR mode

    Explore at 1:1 scale or teleport, with the mapper and material tools.

  • Tablet remote

    Control it from a tablet through a web app linked to the Unreal executable.
