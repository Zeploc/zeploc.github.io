Project · Team project · university, expanded

UnderForge

An online co-op blacksmithing game in the spirit of Overcooked. Gather ores and wood, then smelt, forge, hammer and assemble blades, hilts and guards into swords to fill customer orders, while waves of enemies attack the forge.

Type
Team project
Started
August 2018, at university
Expanded
2020
Engine
Unreal Engine 4
Players
Online co-op over Steam

What I did

  • The modular weapon system: blades, hilts and guards assembled into Broadswords and Kris swords
  • Networking and sync for the forge, anvil, smeltery, workbench, carpentry station and dropped items
  • Orders and customers who take their weapon and walk away
  • Waves and levels, the end-of-day stats and the incinerator
  • Station UI and the menus

The game

  • Stations

    Resource piles, smeltery, forge, anvil, carpentry lathe, crafting and work benches, incinerator and the serving bench.

  • Materials

    Coal, copper, iron and tin ores; bronze, copper, iron, steel and tin ingots; and wood.

  • Tools

    A hammer and a caliper for shaping parts.

  • Enemies

    Goblins, cockroaches, babyclops and daddyclops attack during the day.

  • Combat and moves

    A dodge roll, critical hits and throwing items.

  • Progress

    Levels, waves, currency and stats at the end of each day.

Development

  1. University sprint

    Built by a team at Media Design School in August and September 2018.

  2. Expansion

    Picked up again in March and April 2020: networking, orders, waves and UI.

  3. Update

    Updated to Unreal Engine 4.27 in 2025, with a neater UI and end-game fixes.
