Project · Skyward Studios · Android

Trithym

A mobile arcade game: coloured enemies close in from every side, and you swipe to change colour and match each one before it reaches you.

Studio
Skyward Studios
Platform
Android
Engine
Unity (C#)
Years
2018 – 2019, during university
Status
Not released

About the project

Swipe up, down, left or right to switch between four colours. Tap the currency that drops to collect it, spend it in the shop on Zen and Slow-mo abilities, and chase a high score on a Google Play leaderboard. A second mode, Fall, drops the enemies from above.

What I built

  • The menu design and layout, and the shop
  • The save and load system and the purchase system
  • Enemy pulsing and particle effects
  • Swipe hints, the ability UI and the end screen
  • Google Play services and the leaderboard
